# Policy — Auto Loan (AL-002)

**Eligibility**
- Minimum age 18.
- Vehicle age at loan maturity ≤ 10 years.
- Loan-to-value (LTV) ≤ **1.25** (includes tax, title, GAP).
- Maximum back-end DTI after the new loan: **0.50**.
- Minimum verifiable annual income: $20,000.

**Pricing tiers** (new / used in parens where different)

| Credit score | Max APR | Max LTV |
|---|---|---|
| 740+         | 7.99% (8.49%)  | 1.25 |
| 670–739      | 11.99% (12.99%) | 1.20 |
| 620–669      | 15.99% (17.49%) | 1.15 |
| 580–619      | 19.99% (21.99%) | 1.10 |
| < 580        | Refer-to-human or decline | — |

**Hard declines**
- OFAC / sanctions hit.
- Salvage / branded title.
- Vehicle outside the approved make/model list (configurable).

**Required stipulations**
- Bill of sale or dealer purchase order.
- Proof of insurance with the lender named as loss payee.
- Most recent 2 paystubs.
- Government-issued photo ID.

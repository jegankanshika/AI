# Policy — Unsecured Personal Loan (PL-001)

**Eligibility**
- Minimum age 18 (19 in AL, NE; 21 in MS).
- US permanent resident or citizen.
- Minimum verifiable annual income: $24,000.
- Maximum back-end DTI after the new loan: **0.45**.
- No active Chapter 7/13 bankruptcy. Discharged bankruptcies must be ≥ 36 months old.

**Pricing tiers**

| Credit score | Max APR | Max amount | Max term |
|---|---|---|---|
| 740+         | 14.99% | $50,000 | 60 mo |
| 670–739      | 19.99% | $35,000 | 60 mo |
| 620–669      | 24.99% | $20,000 | 48 mo |
| 580–619      | 29.99% | $10,000 | 36 mo |
| < 580        | Refer-to-human or decline | — | — |

**Hard declines**
- OFAC / sanctions hit.
- Active fraud flag on the applicant.
- Two or more 90+ DPD in the last 12 months.

**Required stipulations**
- Most recent 2 paystubs (or 2 years of 1040 if self-employed).
- Most recent 60 days of bank statements.
- Government-issued photo ID (verified via IDP).
